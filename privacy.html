Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 June 2026 — draft

This Privacy Policy explains how LoL Fantasy ("we", "the app") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile and web applications. By using LoL Fantasy you agree to the practices described here.

Who we are

LoL Fantasy is an independent fan project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Riot Games, Inc. For any privacy question you can reach us at giustino.jose.graterol@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account information — when you create an account, we store your email address, a display name, and an optional avatar. Passwords are handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and are never stored by us in plaintext.
  • Profile preferences — your favorite regions, roles, and similar in-app preferences.
  • Fantasy activity — the rosters you draft, the leagues you join, and your fantasy scores.
  • Technical data — basic device and request metadata (operating system, app version, error logs) used to keep the service stable and secure.

How we use it

  • To run the core features of the app (auth, fantasy leagues, profile).
  • To improve reliability and fix bugs.
  • To communicate with you about your account or material changes to the service.

Esports data

Stats about pro players, teams, tournaments, champions, and patches come from public esports sources (Riot's Data Dragon CDN, the Leaguepedia Cargo API, and similar public datasets). This is public information about competitive matches — it does not describe you as a user of the app.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with the service providers that make the app work — our hosting and authentication provider (Supabase) and our distribution platforms (Apple App Store, Google Play, the web). Each is bound by their own terms.

Children

LoL Fantasy is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your account data at any time by emailing us. We will respond within a reasonable timeframe and in any case within the period required by applicable law.

Security

We use industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS) and rely on Supabase's managed authentication and database security. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information against loss, misuse, and unauthorized access.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves. Material changes will be announced in the app or by email before they take effect.

Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns: giustino.jose.graterol@gmail.com.
