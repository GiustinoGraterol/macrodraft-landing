For League of Legends fans

Esports stats and a free fantasy mode, in one app.

MacroDraft is a community app for League of Legends esports fans. Browse profiles of pro players and teams across the major regions, follow patch changes and the champion tier list, and play a free fantasy mode against friends — no purchases, no wagering.

See what's inside Legal & disclaimers

What's inside

Independent stats, patch tracking, and a friends-only fantasy mode for League of Legends esports — all in a single app on iOS, Android, and the web.

1

Esports profiles

Browse pro players and teams across the major regional leagues. Win/loss records, KDA, CS/min, vision and kill participation — sourced from public esports databases.

2

Patches & tier list

Track every game patch with buffs, nerfs and adjustments per champion, plus a community-oriented tier list by role and lane.

3

Free fantasy mode

Draft a roster of pro players and compete with friends across a tournament. Free to play — no purchases, no wagering, no real-money prizes.
